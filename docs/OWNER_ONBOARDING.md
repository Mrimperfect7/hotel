# Hotel Owner Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Guruvayoor Stay 🛕 — this guide takes you from sign-up to your first
confirmed booking.

## 1. Create an owner account

1. Open **List Your Hotel** → *Create an owner account* → choose **“I own a hotel”**.
2. Verify your email/phone (credentials let you sign in on web and the owner app).

## 2. Register your property (10 steps)

The wizard collects exactly what we verify:

1. **Owner** — your name, phone, WhatsApp, email, GSTIN (optional).
2. **Property** — name, description, address, pincode, check-in/out times.
3. **Location** — lat/lng (drop a pin in Google Maps and copy coordinates).
   We compute your **distance to Guruvayoor Temple automatically** — guests filter by it.
4. **Rooms** — each category: name, price/night, total rooms, bed type, capacity, AC.
5. **Amenities** — Wi-Fi, parking, breakfast, family rooms, lift, temple view…
6. **Pricing** — you control rates; commission is transparent and shown per booking.
7. **Photos** — exterior, lobby, and one photo per room category.
8. **Documents** — business registration, GST certificate (if any), owner ID.
9. **Bank details** — for payouts. Encrypted at rest, never shown publicly.
10. **Submit** → status becomes **“Under admin verification.”**

## 3. Verification

Our team checks documents, location and photos — usually within 24–48 hours.
You'll get a notification when approved (or a message asking for changes).
**Until approval, your property is not publicly visible or bookable.**

## 4. Your dashboard

| Section | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Today's bookings, check-ins/outs, pending requests, revenue |
| **Bookings** | Confirm / reject / cancel; call or WhatsApp guests directly |
| **Availability** | Month calendar of booked vs available rooms per category |
| **Rooms & Pricing** | Change rates, block rooms for maintenance, add categories |
| **Hotel Profile** | Description, rules, contact details |
| **Reviews** | Read guest reviews and post public responses |

## 5. How a booking flows

1. Guest requests rooms → **you get a push notification + dashboard alert.**
2. You **Confirm** (guest is notified instantly) or **Reject** with a reason.
3. Guest pays online (Razorpay) or at the hotel — your payout is the booking
   amount minus platform commission, shown per booking.
4. Guest cancels → rooms return to your inventory automatically.
5. After checkout, the guest can review their stay — respond to build trust.

## 6. Mobile

The owner dashboard is fully responsive — confirm bookings from your phone.
Push notifications are delivered for every new booking request when the app is installed.

## Payouts & commission

- Commission is **configurable** (platform default 10%, hotel-specific overrides possible).
- Example: ₹2,000 room → platform ₹200 → **you receive ₹1,800** per night.
- Your per-booking payout is always visible in the bookings table.
